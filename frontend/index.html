          WATTZAN

          Project purpose

          Why WATTZAN was developed

          The system helps researchers and planners interpret expected electricity demand across Sultan Kudarat through municipality-level forecasts and transparent operational indicators.

          • Forecast short-term daily electricity consumption.
          • Estimate peak demand and capacity utilization.
          • Classify normal, elevated, high, and critical conditions.
          • Produce explainable recommendations for planning and preparedness.

          From location selection to decision support

          Four-stage workflow
          1. 1
            Select location

            Choose a municipality or city through the interactive mapping controls.

          2. 2
            Prepare inputs

            Combine date-matched weather, population, holiday, load, and optional capacity inputs.

          3. 3
            Run models

            Generate municipality-aware MLR, SARIMA, hybrid, and peak-demand results.

          4. 4
            Interpret output

            Review demand level, stability, reasons, recommendations, and planning charts.
